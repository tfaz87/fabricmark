Why FabricMark exists

Clothes should come with honest answers

The fashion industry thrives on opacity. We exist to change that — publishing independent assessments of fabric quality, ethical practices and supply-chain transparency so that you can buy with your eyes open. All profits after operating costs go to charities supporting fast fashion workers and animals.
